# Squash Score privacy policy

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

Squash Score doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.

- Match scores and results are kept on your Apple Watch and iPhone only.
- When you finish a match, the result is sent directly from your watch to your
  own iPhone using Apple's Watch Connectivity. Nothing is sent to us or to
  anyone else.
- The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- If you delete the app, its match history is deleted with it.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

Questions? [Open an issue](https://github.com/tonybfox/squash/issues/new) on
GitHub.
