**Description** [4000]

```
Squash Score keeps the score of your squash match on your Apple Watch, so you can play instead of trying to remember whether it's 8–6 or 6–8.

TAP TO SCORE
The screen is split in two: blue on the left is you, red on the right is your opponent. Tap a side to give them the rally. The numbers are big enough to read at a glance between points.

KNOWS THE RULES
Games use point-a-rally scoring to 11, win by 2, so 12–10 and 15–13 are handled for you. When a game is won, the scores reset, a dot is added under the winner, and they serve first in the next game.

FOLLOWS THE SERVE
Your first tap picks who serves first. After that a dot at the top of the screen always shows who's serving: whoever won the last rally.

FIX MISTAKES
Tapped the wrong side? The small minus button takes the last point back, and the serve goes back to where it was. It even works across the end of a game.

FINISH AND SEE WHO WON
Tap the flag to finish the match and see the winner and the games score. Tap again to start a fresh match.

YOUR MATCH HISTORY ON IPHONE
Every finished match is sent to your iPhone. See your won and lost record, and every match with its date, games score and the points in each game. Your phone doesn't need to be nearby: results arrive the next time you open the iPhone app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your matches stay on your watch and your iPhone.

Requires an Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. The iPhone app needs iOS 17 or later.
```

## App Review Information

**Sign-in required:** No

**Notes:**

```
Squash Score is a scoreboard for a squash match, used on Apple Watch. The iPhone app carries the watch app and shows the history of matches finished on the watch.

To try it on the watch: tap either side to pick who serves first, then tap a side for each rally that side wins. A game ends at 11 points with a 2-point lead. The minus button in each top corner takes a point back. The flag button at the bottom finishes the match and shows the winner; tap that screen to start again.

Finishing a match after at least one completed game sends the result to the iPhone app, where it appears in the match list.
```
