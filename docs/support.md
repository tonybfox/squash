# Squash Score support

Squash Score keeps the score of a squash match on your Apple Watch, and keeps
a history of finished matches on your iPhone.

## Getting help

Found a bug or have a question?
[Open an issue](https://github.com/tonybfox/squash/issues/new) on GitHub and
include your watch model and watchOS version.

## Common questions

**How do I install it on my watch?**
It usually installs on its own after you get the iPhone app. If it doesn't,
open the **Watch** app on your iPhone, scroll down to **Available Apps** and
tap **Install** next to Squash. You can also get it straight from the App
Store on your watch.

**How do I keep score?**
Blue on the left is you, red on the right is your opponent. Your first tap
picks who serves first. After that, tap the side that won each rally. Games
are point-a-rally to 11, win by 2.

**I tapped the wrong side.**
Tap the small minus button in the top corner of that side to take its last
point back. The serve goes back to who had it before.

**How do I finish a match?**
Tap the flag button at the bottom of the screen. You'll see the winner and the
games score. Tap that screen again to start a new match.

**A match isn't showing on my iPhone.**
Open the Squash app on your iPhone; results finished away from your phone
arrive the next time you open it. Matches finished before a single game was
completed aren't saved.

**My watch goes back to the clock face mid-match.**
On your watch, go to **Settings → General → Return to Clock** and choose
**After 1 hour**, or pick Squash in the list there and turn on
**Return to App**.

**How do I delete a match?**
In the iPhone app, swipe left on the match and tap **Delete**.
